Project overview · branch C in progress

mdbench

A method-ladder benchmark for molecular simulation. Four scientific questions, each answered at five levels of rigour, on systems with published experimental data, all on one 6 GB laptop GPU.

Nothing here predicts anything new. Each calculation is scored against measured values (ITC binding data, crystal endpoints, smFRET distributions, mutant kcat series), and its wall-clock cost is recorded beside the score. Plotting accuracy against log cost for each branch shows where extra rigour stops paying.

“For this system class, rigour above Ln did not pay.” One such sentence per branch is the product of the repo.

The ladder

Four questions × five levels of rigour

Each row is a branch; each column adds cost and physical content. L0 is a deliberately crude null model that every level above it must beat. Branch C is built first: it is the cheapest, and it exercises the machinery (PLUMED, collective variables, convergence diagnostics, the results schema) that the other three depend on.

Alanine dipeptide: free energy relative to C7eq/β, by method

Each panel has its own scale. Rows with no point mean the method never sampled that basin. Cost is wall-clock on the RTX 4050; the MSM's cost includes the L2 trajectories it is built from. L0 and L1 use GBn2 implicit solvent; L2 and L3 use explicit TIP3P. To see the L2 and metadynamics runs play out, open the motion page.

  • Three methods agree on αR. Unbiased MD, metadynamics and the MSM all give ΔG ≈ 0.47–0.48 kcal/mol, about 30% population.
  • The rare basin needed L3. 50 ns of unbiased MD never visited αL (≈3%). Metadynamics and umbrella sampling agree on it at ≈1.9 kcal/mol.
  • Umbrella αR is unconverged. The bias is on φ only, and ψ stays slow inside each window: the two halves of the data give 21% and 5% αR. This is a CV-choice failure, as the intent file anticipated.
  • MSM error bars are too small. ±0.02 kcal/mol is about 3× below the spread across replicates. A bootstrap over whole trajectories is pending.
  • The nulls land close. L0 and L1 cost about 2 minutes and fall within 0.2 kcal/mol of the explicit-water answer for both basins. Their error bars cover only the choice of starting structures.
  • AdK angles separate cleanly. θNMP is 72.6° open and 44.1° closed; θLID is 145.7° and 106.2°. The L0 null calls apo AdK fully closed, which is almost certainly wrong, as a null is allowed to be.

Branches

Scope and finish line

CConformational distributions

Makes the ensemble the computed object. Alanine dipeptide first, then metadynamics and umbrella sampling on the ADK domain coordinate, then an MSM sharing trajectories with B.

Done when
  • Alanine dipeptide FES matches published surfaces
  • Metadynamics and umbrella FES for ADK agree, or the gap is explained
  • MSM with validated implied timescales gives populations

Open: how much simulation does the ADK FES need before it stops moving?

ABinding affinity

Relative and absolute binding free energies at every level. CB7 to debug alchemy in hours, then T4L L99A as the real benchmark; a congeneric RBFE series only if both land.

Done when
  • L0–L3 run on CB7 and T4L with uncertainty and wall-clock
  • Spearman, pairwise accuracy and RMSE with bootstrap CIs
  • Flattening point named; disk under 40 GB

Open: is L2→L3 worth 100× the compute for ranking specifically?

BConformational change

Does each method recover a known motion? Adenylate kinase open/closed, plus fast folders (Trp-cage, chignolin) whose reversible folding is reachable on this GPU.

Done when
  • ANM motion for ADK scored before any MD runs
  • Replicate MD, with the number that saw a transition recorded
  • Protein-only XTC, branch under 30 GB

Open: does seconds of ANM capture most of what microseconds of MD show?

DEnzymatic properties

Reproduces measured structure–activity relationships: KSI active-site field vs kcat across mutants, and the chorismate mutase barrier with distal-mutation data.

Done when
  • KSI field–kcat correlation reproduced with a CI
  • Chorismate mutase PMF at L3 and at L4 (xTB QM/MM)
  • Unreproduced distal effects written up as results

Open: does a non-polarisable force field get the KSI field right at all?

House rules

What keeps the comparison fair

  • L0 before L3. No expensive run until the null has a recorded score.
  • Ground truth is adopted, not curated. Every reference value carries a DOI.
  • Ranking first. Spearman ρ and pairwise accuracy outweigh RMSE.
  • Noise floor ~0.4 kcal/mol. No claimed resolution below experimental reproducibility.
  • One parquet row per calculation. Results are kept; trajectories are disposable.
  • Hardware held fixed. 6 GB VRAM ceiling; any rented run records its GPU.
